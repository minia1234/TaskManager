Hardware sensor libraries
========================================================================
TaskMonitor calls the NVIDIA (nvml.dll, nvapi64.dll) and AMD (atiadlxx.dll)
libraries that come with the graphics driver installed on your PC, and reads
the HWiNFO shared-memory interface when HWiNFO is running. None of these are
included in TaskMonitor.
